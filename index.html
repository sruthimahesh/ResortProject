About FOREST NEST

Forest Nest was crafted with deep reverence for our surroundings. Every architectural line, every eco-friendly material, and every panoramic window was designed to blur the boundary between indoor luxury and outdoor wonder. We wanted to build more than just a resort; we wanted to create an immersive eco-haven where sustainable living meets soul-stirring comfort. Today, Forest Nest stands as a testament to that vision. When you step onto our grounds, you are no longer a mere spectator of nature—you are a part of it. Whether you are waking up to the symphony of morning birds in our luxury treehouses, breathing in the scent of pine on a guided forest trek, or watching the stars come alive by a crackling bonfire, you are experiencing the wilderness in its purest, most comfortable form.
