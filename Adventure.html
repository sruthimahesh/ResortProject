Nature & Adventure Experiences

Forest Nest's wilderness is your playground—from canopy-level thrills to stream-side explorations

Hanging Bridge Walk

Walk 150 feet above the forest floor on our iconic suspended bridge. Morning mist, bird songs, and valley views create an unforgettable sensory experience.

Zip Line Through Canopy

Soar 300 meters through three forest zones at 40 km/hour. Feel your heart race as you fly like Wayanad's birds through changing ecosystems.

Infinity Pool Experience

A naturally fed pool that stays at the hills’ own temperature, cool & refreshing, with panoramic views across the valley. Separate children’s section and sun loungers.

Archery Experience

Learn traditional bow and arrow techniques in our forest-surrounded range. Connection to Kerala's Kalaripayattu martial arts heritage.

Stream Walk & Waterfall

Wade through crystal-clear Karanchi stream to our 40-foot artificial waterfall. Safe swimming in natural pool setting with lifeguard supervision.

Guided Nature Trails

Nature walk with resort team through diverse trails. Learn to identify 50+ plants, spot rare butterflies, and understand Western Ghats ecology.
